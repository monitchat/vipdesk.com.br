When your business WhatsApp becomes a bottleneck

Many customers prefer to reach businesses on WhatsApp, but a single shared phone was never built for a whole team. The symptoms are usually the same:

  • One phone passed from hand to hand, or several personal numbers speaking for the company.
  • Conversations without an owner: two people answer the same customer, or nobody does.
  • When an agent goes on vacation or leaves the company, the conversation history leaves too.
  • Nobody knows how long a customer has been waiting or which requests are overdue.
  • Quote requests get lost between messages and never reach the sales team.
  • Managers can’t follow the operation without reading conversation after conversation.

How VipDesk organizes conversations

Every message follows a clear path, from first contact to resolution or sale.

  1. The conversation enters a shared queue

    WhatsApp messages — as well as Instagram, Facebook, Telegram and email — arrive in the same queue, visible to authorized team members.

  2. An agent picks it up with the full history

    Whoever takes over sees everything that already happened with that contact, including conversations that started on other channels or were handled by teammates.

  3. Requests become tickets with deadlines

    When a request needs follow-up, the agent opens a ticket straight from the conversation, with a category and first-response and resolution deadlines.

  4. Buying intent becomes an opportunity

    If the conversation is a sale, it moves into your CRM pipeline, with proposals, contracts and automatic follow-ups.

How your WhatsApp number is connected

A number can be connected to the platform by scanning a QR code. This method relies on WhatsApp Web–based interfaces that are not approved by Meta, so it carries a risk of the number being blocked or disconnected at WhatsApp's discretion.

For business-critical operations, our Terms of Use recommend the official WhatsApp Business API (Cloud API), which is subject to Meta's own rules and fees. During the demo, our sales team will help you choose the best path for your volume and type of operation.

Frequently asked questions

Can several agents use the same WhatsApp number?

Yes. Conversations from the number land in a shared queue and each authorized agent can pick up a contact with the full history. The number of users depends on your plan; our sales team can walk you through the available options.

What happens to the history when an agent leaves the company?

The history is stored per contact on the platform, not on the phone of whoever handled it. Just remove the user's access: the conversations remain available to whoever takes over.

Can I handle Instagram, Facebook and email on the same screen?

Yes. WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook, Telegram and email arrive in the same queue, with a unified history per contact. See omnichannel customer service.

How do I make sure customers get answered on time?

With SLA-based tickets: each ticket has a first-response and a resolution deadline, an alert before it expires and automatic escalation. For leads, there is a first-contact deadline and reassignment when the rep doesn't respond.

Can a conversation turn into a sale?

Yes. The conversation can move into your sales pipeline, with proposals, contracts, goals and automatic follow-ups. Learn more about the WhatsApp CRM.

Is there a risk of the number being blocked?

With the QR code connection, yes: it is not a Meta-approved method and WhatsApp may block the number at its discretion, as detailed in our Terms of Use. For business-critical operations, the recommendation is the official WhatsApp Business API. Respecting contacts' consent (opt-in) and avoiding unsolicited bulk messages is also mandatory.
