Calendar integration for your sales team

VipDesk connects to Google Calendar and Microsoft 365 / Teams so that appointments booked on the platform respect the real calendar of the person who will attend.

Real availability

Before offering a time slot to a customer, the platform checks when the professional's calendar is already busy.

  • We read only the start and end of events
  • We do not access titles, descriptions, attendees or locations
  • No event from your calendar is copied into our database

Meetings created automatically

When a customer books a time, the event shows up on the professional's calendar — nobody has to copy anything by hand.

  • Invitation sent to the customer
  • Google Meet or Microsoft Teams link included in the invite
  • Reschedules and cancellations reflected in the same event

The connection is authorized by the user and can be revoked at any time, in the platform or directly in the Google or Microsoft account. Details on how data is handled are in our Privacy Policy.

Frequently asked questions

What is VipDesk?

VipDesk is an omnichannel customer service and CRM platform built by Vipphone Software, a software company based in Brazil. It brings WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook, Telegram, email and phone conversations into a single screen, and keeps customer service, SLA-based tickets, your sales pipeline, automations and your team's calendar in the same place.

How do multiple agents share the same WhatsApp number?

Conversations land in a shared queue and any authorized agent can pick up a contact with the full history, including conversations from other channels. Access is controlled by user role and actions are recorded in an audit log. See WhatsApp customer service for teams.

Can I bring different channels together?

Yes. WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook, Telegram and email arrive in the same queue with a unified history per contact, and phone is part of the same platform. Learn more about omnichannel customer service.

How does the CRM work?

The CRM has configurable pipelines, proposals, contracts and goals. Leads can be distributed automatically across sales reps, with a deadline for first contact and reassignment when nobody responds. See the WhatsApp CRM.

What role does AI play?

AI helps with triage, summarizes conversations, suggests replies and helps classify sales opportunities. Your agent stays in charge of what gets sent. More on AI for customer service.

How is a WhatsApp number connected?

A number can be connected by scanning a QR code. This method relies on WhatsApp Web–based interfaces that are not approved by Meta and carries a real risk of the number being blocked, as explained in our Terms of Use, which recommend the official WhatsApp Business API for business-critical operations. During the demo, our sales team will help you choose the right option for your operation.

How can I try VipDesk?

The platform may be offered with a free trial for a limited period, as described in our Terms of Use. To get a trial or learn about plans, email our sales team at comercial@vipphone.com.br.

How do I book a demo?

Click “Book a demo” or email comercial@vipphone.com.br with the channels you use, your team size and your time zone. The demo is built around your own channels and pipeline.

How is data protected?

VipDesk is operated in compliance with Brazil's General Data Protection Law (LGPD), with encrypted integration credentials, role-based access and audit logs. Controller and processor roles are described in our Privacy Policy.
