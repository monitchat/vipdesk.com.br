What is omnichannel customer service?

Omnichannel customer service means every contact channel your business offers works as one continuous conversation with the customer. Whether they sent a message on Instagram, wrote an email or reached out on WhatsApp, whoever responds sees the full history and picks up where the conversation left off.

In practice, this requires a platform that receives every channel in the same queue, identifies the contact and keeps the history together. That's exactly what VipDesk does.

Multichannel vs. omnichannel

Being present on many channels is not the same as serving customers in an integrated way.

MultichannelOmnichannel
ChannelsSeveral channels, each with its own toolSeveral channels on one platform
HistorySplit by channelUnified per contact
QueueOne queue per channel or deviceOne queue for the whole team
Customer experienceRepeats their story every time they switch channelsContinues the conversation where it left off
ManagementFragmented view of the operationDeadlines and results tracked in one place

Continuity and team management

Continuity of service

Customers don’t have to repeat themselves when they switch channels or agents.

  • Unified history per contact
  • Tickets opened straight from the conversation, with a category
  • Conversations stored on the platform, not on a phone

Operations management

Clear deadlines and responsibilities for the whole team.

  • First-response and resolution deadlines (SLAs)
  • Alerts before deadlines expire and automatic escalation
  • Role-based access and an audit log of user actions

Business benefits

  • Fewer forgotten customers: a single queue with deadlines and alerts instead of several inboxes.
  • Sales connected to support: a conversation can become an opportunity in the CRM without retyping data.
  • A more productive team: automations and AI take care of repetitive tasks.
  • Less dependence on specific people: what your team knows about each customer is recorded.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between multichannel and omnichannel customer service?

In multichannel, a business is present on several channels, but each one works in isolation. In omnichannel, channels are integrated: the team handles everything from one queue and sees each customer's unified history.

Which channels does VipDesk bring together?

WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook, Telegram, email and phone.

Is the history from different channels kept together?

Yes. History is unified per contact, so the agent sees the whole conversation no matter which channel it started on.

Does omnichannel customer service also help sales?

Yes. In VipDesk, customer service and the CRM live on the same platform: a conversation can become an opportunity in the pipeline, with automatic lead distribution and follow-ups.

How do I track service deadlines?

With SLA-based tickets, which have first-response and resolution deadlines, alerts before they expire and automatic escalation.
